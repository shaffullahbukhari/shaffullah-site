What AI assistants look for

Five things decide whether an AI can use your page.

The free audit checks about 34 signals in these five areas and tells you, in plain English, what to change.

1

Can AI crawlers get in?

Whether ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google and Bing are allowed to read the page, and whether it's readable without running JavaScript.

2

Does it answer questions?

Question-style headings in English and Spanish, with short, direct answers an assistant can quote.

3

Can machines tell who you are?

Structured data that states your firm's name, practice areas, languages, location and official profiles.

4

Is it worth trusting?

Who wrote or reviewed it, when it was last updated, contact and privacy pages, and sources for facts.

5

Is it summarised well?

Titles, descriptions, canonical links and preview tags that describe each page correctly.

Proof on my own site

74 to 98, with the same audit I'll run for you.

Before this rebuild, shaffullah.com scored 74 out of 100. These changes took it to 98:

  • Business and FAQ markup, so assistants know who I am and what I answer
  • Short, direct answers under question headings
  • A robots.txt that lets AI search crawlers in, plus a sitemap and an llms.txt
  • Author name and a visible “last updated” date
  • Contact and privacy pages linked from every page
  • Shorter titles and descriptions that say exactly what each page is

Questions firms ask

What is AEO?

AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) is making a website easy for AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude and Google AI Overviews to find, understand and quote, so your firm can be named when someone asks them for an immigration lawyer.

How is AEO different from SEO?

AEO builds on SEO. Both need a fast, crawlable, trustworthy site. AEO adds question-shaped content, short direct answers, structured data and making sure AI crawlers are allowed in, because AI assistants quote passages instead of listing pages.

Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend my firm?

No one honestly can. AI assistants decide for themselves. AEO makes sure that when they look, your site is easy to find, trust and quote.

What does the free AEO audit include?

A 0–100 score for a page of your website across about 34 checks in five areas, which AI crawlers can read it, the fixes that matter most, and ready-to-paste code such as business markup and an llms.txt file.
