What happens on a call

Four steps, the same way every time.

1

Answers in their language

Greets callers with your firm's name, says it's an AI assistant, and switches to the caller's language.

2

Takes the basics

Name, callback number, what the case is about, and any court date or deadline coming up.

3

Books or escalates

Books a consultation. A detention or a hearing this week goes straight to your on-call attorney by text.

4

Writes it up

Your team gets a short summary in English for every call, ready when the office opens.

Questions firms ask

Does it give legal advice?

No. It collects intake basics and hands anything legal to your team. It says so to callers if they ask.

Which languages does it speak?

Spanish, English, Portuguese, Arabic, French and Haitian Creole. Other languages can be set up on request.

Do callers know it's an AI?

Yes. It says so at the start of every call, in line with ABA Formal Opinion 512.

Where do summaries and alerts go?

Summaries go to the email address you choose. Urgent calls also go by text to your on-call attorney.

What does it cost?

A fixed monthly fee, quoted after the test run once you've seen it work for your firm.
