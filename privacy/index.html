Privacy & email

What I collect, why, and how to switch it off.

Short version: I ask for your email so a copy of your free report reaches you. Marketing emails only go to people who tick a separate box, and every one has an unsubscribe link.

Who I am

This site and its free report tools are run by Shaffullah Bukhari, who builds AI systems for US immigration law firms. I'm not a law firm and nothing here is legal advice. Questions about your data: hello@shaffullah.com.

Free reports

When you request an Impersonation Exposure Report or an AEO audit, I collect what you type in the form:

  • the website you want checked, and for the Exposure Report the firm name, any attorney names and your answers to three quick questions;
  • your email address, so a copy of the report can be sent to you;
  • a one-way, scrambled version of your internet address, only to limit how many reports one connection can run per hour.

The report itself is built from public information only: domain registration records, public certificate logs, DNS and the public pages of the websites involved. It never asks for client files or client details.

Your report has a private link that only works for people you share it with. I get a copy of each request so I can follow up personally, usually once, to offer to walk you through it.

Occasional emails (only if you say yes)

Every form has a separate, unticked box for occasional emails about new scams targeting immigration firms, AI visibility and my services. If you don't tick it, you only get what you asked for: your report, or a reply to your request.

If you tick it, I record when and on which form you agreed. Every occasional email has an unsubscribe link, and you can also just reply “unsubscribe”. Joining the Learn waitlist means you agree to get the field notes and workshop news; you can leave the same way.

Test runs and other requests

If you ask for a test run of the 24/7 line, a founding place or anything else, I keep your name, firm, email and what you wrote so I can reply and set it up.

Services that help run this site

  • Hosting: this site is a static website on GitHub Pages. The report tools run on computers I control.
  • Form delivery: until the report tools are fully automated, the forms on this site are delivered to my inbox by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which also sends you an automatic confirmation.
  • Email delivery: reports and replies are sent from hello@shaffullah.com through my email provider.
  • Live demo call: when you press “Start talking” on the 24/7 line page, your voice is processed by ElevenLabs, the voice-AI provider that runs the demo agent. Nothing happens until you press the button and allow your microphone.
  • Demo video: the walkthrough video is hosted by Loom.
  • Booking: call bookings go through Calendly.
  • Fonts: fonts load from Google Fonts.

Each of these services handles data under its own privacy policy. I don't sell or rent your information, and this site doesn't use advertising trackers.

Keeping and deleting your data

I keep report requests and contact details for as long as they're useful for following up and for running the service. Email hello@shaffullah.com and I'll delete your details and reports, usually within a few days.
