    Questions firms ask

    Straight answers.

    What is lawyer impersonation and why are immigration firms targeted?

    Scammers copy a real immigration lawyer's name, photos and videos on fake pages, ads, WhatsApp numbers and lookalike websites, then ask frightened families for money. Immigration clients are targeted because they are often under time pressure, speak Spanish or another language first, and may not know how to check what is real.

    What does the free Impersonation Exposure Report check?

    It checks up to 600 lookalike versions of your web address, new websites using your name in public certificate logs, whether clients can verify your real channels on your website, your email spoofing protection (DMARC), and where to report each finding. It uses public information only.

    Does the 24/7 line give legal advice?

    No. It says it is an AI at the start of every call, collects intake basics, books consultations and sends urgent calls such as detentions to a person. Anything legal goes to your team.

    What is AEO?

    AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) makes a website easy for AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude and Google AI Overviews to find, understand and quote, so your firm can be named when a family asks them for an immigration lawyer.

    Do you need access to client files?

    No. Every service works from public information and your written authorization. The verification page confirms official channels, never who your clients are, in line with ABA Model Rule 1.6.
